# Project Risk Analysis
## University Library Management System

### 1. Introduction

Project risk analysis is the process of identifying possible problems that may affect project success. It helps the project team prepare solutions to reduce or manage these risks.

### 2. Risk Assessment Scale

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Small chance of occurrence or minor impact |
| Medium | Moderate chance of occurrence or impact |
| High | High chance of occurrence or serious impact |

### 3. Risk Identification and Assessment

| Risk ID | Project Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Loss of project files | Medium | High | Regularly save and commit files to GitHub. |
| R-02 | Lack of time to complete tasks | Medium | High | Follow the project schedule and prioritize important tasks. |
| R-03 | Incorrect or incomplete requirements | Medium | High | Review requirements and confirm stakeholder needs. |
| R-04 | GitHub configuration problems | Low | Medium | Learn basic Git commands and keep backup copies. |
| R-05 | Errors in UML diagrams | Medium | Medium | Review diagrams and compare them with the SRS. |
| R-06 | UI prototype usability problems | Medium | Medium | Test the prototype with classmates and collect feedback. |
| R-07 | Lack of stakeholder feedback | Medium | High | Consult available students and library staff. |
| R-08 | Incomplete project documentation | Medium | High | Review the assignment checklist before submission. |

### 4. Risk Management Strategy

The following actions will be taken to manage project risks:

- Maintain regular backups of project documents.
- Follow the planned project schedule.
- Review requirements before preparing UML diagrams.
- Check all diagrams for errors and consistency.
- Test the UI prototype with classmates.
- Review all deliverables before final submission.

### 5. Conclusion

Risk analysis helps identify possible problems before they affect the project. By applying suitable mitigation strategies, the University Library Management System project can be completed more effectively and within the planned schedule.
